One home. One app.

Less admin.
More living.

Split bills, share chores, build shopping lists, and keep every house update in one calm place.

01

Split bills, skip the awkward math

Create an invoice, share it with the house, and let Awa work out everyone’s share. When they pay, you simply approve it.

02

Chores don’t do themselves

Create tasks, assign them to any roommate, and check them off when they’re done. Everyone always knows whose turn it is.

03

All talk, no clutter

Keep rent reminders, chore updates, and the occasional meme together, so nothing important disappears into another group chat.

04

Lists everyone can agree on. Mostly.

Share groceries and essentials in real time. At checkout, Awa can turn the list into a bill and split the cost automatically.
